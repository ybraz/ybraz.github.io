I'm **Yuri Braz**, a security architect with 15+ years in the field, the last five at the largest private bank in Latin America.

Inside the same organization I've owned **four distinct technical domains** — application security, cloud security, vulnerability and exposure management, and pipeline security with cloud IAM governance — after coming up through networking, data center, cryptography and Kubernetes. That breadth is deliberate, not accidental: security architecture that actually holds gets written by someone who has owned each layer it has to stitch together.

If there's a thesis behind my choices, it's this: **as security operations get automated by AI, value migrates toward architecture, judgment, and the strategic communication of risk** — the three things no pipeline replaces.

## Focus areas

- **Architecture & governance** — reference architectures, threat modeling (STRIDE), Zero Trust (NIST 800-207), ISO 27001, NIST CSF, MITRE ATT&CK, arc42, ATAM.
- **Cloud security** — AWS, Azure, GCP, OCI and Huawei Cloud: secure landing zones, CSPM, CIEM, SCPs, multicloud governance.
- **Identity & access** — IAM, non-human identity (NHI) governance, least privilege, permission boundaries, OIDC/OAuth 2.0, workload identity (SPIFFE/SPIRE).
- **AppSec & DevSecOps** — SAST, DAST, SCA, SBOM, software supply chain, CI/CD guardrails, API security.
- **Exposure management** — CTEM, risk-based prioritization, breach and attack simulation, purple team.
- **Automation & platform** — Policy-as-Code (OPA/Rego), Terraform, CloudFormation, Step Functions, Python, Kubernetes/Istio.

## What I write about

I keep a technical series on identity, authorization, and the security of non-human identities and AI agents. The arguments I return to: authorization is not enforcement, scopes are not least privilege, NHI governance is a lifecycle rather than a product you buy, build-time and runtime are not the same control for an autonomous agent — and revocation is an anti-pattern, the cleanup you pay for having no lifecycle.

Outside security I write about philosophy, formation, and the craft of thinking clearly. The two aren't separate: a good share of architecture mistakes are reasoning mistakes before they are technology mistakes.

Most posts on this site are in Portuguese.
